# Water's data

## The sonar

`ping.wav` and `echo.wav`: the sub's sonar, cut from "Submarine Sonar" by
DRAGON-STUDIO, published on Pixabay on 8 August 2025,
pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-submarine-sonar-386154/,
under the Pixabay Content License.

*	`ping.wav`: its first six tenths of a second, the ping and its ring,
	faded in over 5ms and out over its last 150ms so neither end clicks.

*	`echo.wav`: the echo off the rock, its first 0.35s played half again as
	fast, so a fifth higher and shorter, 0.23s, faded out over its last
	100ms.

Each made mono, 16 bits at 22050 a second, and brought to a peak 2dB under
full; the app makes them as loud as it wants them as it loads them, the
ping quieter than the scooter and each echo as near as its rock is.
