# Water's data

## The sonar

The sub's sonar, each under the Pixabay Content License:

*	`ping.wav`: its ping going out, "Submarine Sonar" by DRAGON-STUDIO,
	published on Pixabay on 8 August 2025,
	pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-submarine-sonar-386154/:
	all of it, the ping and its ring dying away of itself, faded in over 5ms
	and out over its last 200ms so neither end clicks.

*	`echo.wav`: an echo coming back off the rock, "Sonar" by VoiceBosch,
	published on Pixabay on 2 January 2024,
	pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-sonar-183436/: the first
	of its eight pings as it is, from just before it to just after, at its
	own level, faded in over 5ms and out over 10ms, so neither end clicks.

Each made mono, 16 bits at 22050 a second, the ping brought to a peak 2dB
under full; the app makes them as loud as it wants them as it loads them, the
ping quieter than the scooter and each echo as near as its rock is.

## The scooter

`prop.wav`: the scooter's whine as the diver sprints, a loop, from
"submarine prop whooshing" by rexen_devar (Freesound), via
freesound_community on Pixabay, published there on 19 June 2022,
pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-submarine-prop-whooshing-45861/,
under the Pixabay Content License. All of it but its last 150ms, which is
faded into its start at equal power, so its end runs on into its start
with no seam, 1.16 seconds; made mono, 16 bits at 22050 a second, and
brought to a peak 2dB under full. The app writes it to a stream as the
diver sprints, rising quickly and dying away after.

## A bump into the rock

`thud.wav`: the diver bumping the rock or a stone, from "Dramatic Thud
Stinger" by DRAGON-STUDIO, published on Pixabay on 5 November 2026,
pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-dramatic-thud-stinger-631487/,
under the Pixabay Content License. Its first 1.2 seconds, the thud and the
start of its ring, faded in over 5ms and its ring faded away over its last
half second, so it dies sooner than it would; made mono, 16 bits at 22050 a
second, and brought to a peak 2dB under full. The app plays it as loud as
the bump was hard.
