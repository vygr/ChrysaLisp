# Press cuttings

Scans of what was written about Taos and Tao Systems at the time, from 1990
to 1996, kept here for the historical record. They are reduced copies of
scans made by Chris Hinsley from his own collection.

Each article is the work of its author and the property of its publisher.
They are credited below as printed on the page. Nothing here is ours but the
subject.

Read with [How We Got Here](../history.md), which quotes from them.

## Notes

*	Three scans are not complete. BYTE July 1994 stops where the article says
	"continued". Waters 1996 is the first page only. One cutting has no
	magazine name or date on it.

*	The second BYTE article of March 1991 and the Personal Computer World item
	of June 1991 are about the PgC7600 processor, not Taos. They are here
	because the chip was being built with Taos in mind.

*	BYTE of March 1991 says that Nik Spicer added the parallel aspects of
	Taos. That was a mistake in the article. He did not, and never said he
	did. See [How We Got Here](../history.md), "Who did what".

*	The file called "IEEE 1992" in the original collection is the IEE Review,
	of the UK's Institution of Electrical Engineers.

*	The Edge cutting is five pages, the editorial, page 3 of the magazine,
	and the four pages of the article. The front cover is a photograph of
	Chris's framed copy, taken in 2026, not a scan.
