# ChrysaLisp 7.4

Films with their sound. Films plays mp4, Matroska and WebM, its pictures
by the host's decoders, and their sound by decoders of our own: AAC with
HE-AAC's SBR and parametric stereo, AC-3 and E-AC-3, MP3 and FLAC. MP3
and FLAC files play as films of their own, a book of 48 hours too. Links
over the internet are sealed, there are voice calls between desktops,
and TLS 1.2 beside 1.3.

This is a tag, not a GitHub release.

**Update every machine together, with `make install`.** The handshake of
a link is now CLDOOR2, and a machine of 7.3 does not link to one of 7.4.
The host programs have new calls, the microphone, streams of sound, the
decoders of pictures on Linux and Windows and the echo canceller, and the
boot image needs them.

## Films and their sound

*	FLM is version 3, a reel of packets, its rate in its header:
	`lib/media/reel.inc` and `lib/media/flm.inc`, the codec in
	`gui/pixmap/flm.vp`. The Films app is a player: play, pause, step, a
	timeline to scrub, shown at 1/2, 1/3 or 1/4, each film played again
	where it was left, and the films that played a button each. Old films
	are made new with `toflm -c`.

*	mp4, `lib/media/mp4.inc`: a film of boxes read as a reel, each track's
	edit list, ctts, QuickTime's sound entries of version 1 and 2. A film
	of two hours is up in 0.65 s. The `mp4` command says what one holds.

*	Pictures by the host: H.264 and H.265 by VideoToolbox on macOS, H.264 by
	V4L2 on Linux and by Media Foundation on Windows.

*	Sound by decoders of our own, in `lib/media/`, kept to the pictures by
	what has been heard, and a step sooner or later by 40 ms kept with each
	film. Of more than two channels the front pair with the centre in it.
	On the M4, a packet or frame:
	*	AAC-LC, `aac.inc`, 0.5 ms; with HE-AAC's SBR, `sbr.inc`, and
		parametric stereo, `ps.inc`, said by the config or found in the
		stream, 1.5 to 1.7 ms. 65 to 78 dB of ffmpeg on its conformance
		streams.
	*	AC-3, 0.77 ms of 5.1, and E-AC-3, 0.59 ms, `ac3.inc`: short blocks,
		spectral extension, the adaptive hybrid transform, dynamic range.
	*	MP3, `mp3.inc`, layer III of MPEG-1, 2 and 2.5, 0.68 ms a frame of
		1152.
	*	FLAC, `flac.inc`, each sample as it was coded, against the
		reference encoder's sources.

*	MP3 and FLAC files are reels too, their frames found as they are
	wanted, not read all at once: a book of 48 hours opens in 1 ms and
	seeks. The picture is the frame's samples.

*	Matroska and WebM, `lib/media/mkv.inc`, read as an mp4's tracks: Films
	plays H.264 and H.265 of them with AAC, AC-3, E-AC-3, MP3 or FLAC.
	A film of ten minutes opens in 155 ms.

*	Native code for the hot parts of the decoders, in
	`lib/media/lisp.vp`, shared where they can be: the bit reader, Huffman
	books by table, the DCT-IV of the IMDCT, a product of a matrix, the
	gather of a column, noise, and each codec's own.

## Audio

*	The microphone. The Audio service sends what it hears, 16 bit mono at
	16000 a second, in pieces of 20ms to whoever listens, and streams play
	a sound as it comes, a voice from far away: the start of voice chat.
	Six new entries in the host's audio table, three for the microphone
	and three for streams, in the SDL3 and ALSA drivers. A listener and a stream
	are held on a lease, so one that has gone lets go of the device. The
	`mic` command. A hidden desktop hears a tone, for the tests.
	[library_primitives.md](../ai_digest/library_primitives.md), "Audio
	Service". New host programs on every machine, Windows too.

*	Sound pipelines, as the stages of an image: a source, filters and a
	sink, each a task, so no whole sound is held. The microphone, a tone and
	a file in; gain, a low pass and a level; a file and the speaker out.
	`lib/audio/`, `.wav` a module of its own as an image's formats are, and
	`stages-run` in `lib/streams/stages.inc`.
	[async_pipelines.md](../ai_digest/async_pipelines.md), "Sound".

*	Voice, `apps/media/voice/`, once Voice Notes: record, stop, play and delete,
	each note named for when it was made, kept in the user's `voice_notes/`.

*	Calls between desktops, `service/voice`, one a desktop: who is talking
	told, mute, headphones, a noise gate and half duplex so speakers do not
	echo, a call recorded, and a sound file played into it. The
	Whiteboard's share has a call of its own, with mute on every board.

*	Echo cancelling, `src/host/aec.h`: blocks in the frequency domain, two
	filters and a residual suppressor, set on recordings of two real
	rooms. Not yet heard live.

## Networks

*	Links over the internet are sealed: each end an X25519 share, HKDF
	keys each way from it and the mesh key, records of ChaCha20-Poly1305
	with counted nonces. Links on a LAN stay plain, and `link -s` seals
	any. `sys/crypto/` has ChaCha20, Poly1305 and the AEAD for VP code.

*	A keyring of mesh keys by name, the `key` command and the Keys app.

*	TLS 1.2 in the client beside 1.3: ECDHE X25519 or P-256,
	ChaCha20-Poly1305 or AES-128-GCM, RSA or ECDSA, the extended master
	secret, and the downgrade sentinel refused. The Model demo fetches
	Spot from CMU by it, on a task of its own, the window drawn meanwhile.

*	A desktop comes first for a machine's port, a session that is not one
	steps aside. `mail-here` and `mail-service` find one of a name on this
	node only if it is there.

## Sync

*	A waypoint goes round the herd by itself. Each machine's sync service
	offers the waypoint its tree is exactly at to the others, as it starts,
	when a machine is new to it or back, when it has taken one, and when
	`waypoint` notes one; each takes what is newer and offers it on.
	`service/sync/spread.lisp`, [sync.md](../ai_digest/sync.md), "The herd
	by itself".

*	A tree with no `.git` that is already exactly the tree at a waypoint
	takes it, only the waypoint noted, and a push from a machine it names,
	made from a tree at its waypoint, notes it there too.

*	A machine the herd has taken to a newer waypoint makes itself new. Its
	member ends, and `rack.sh`, which keeps it in a loop, does `make
	install` and starts it again; what was there is put back if the
	install fails. Only the member, its own nodes, and not on a tree with a
	`.git`. [rack.md](../ai_digest/rack.md), "A member". As it starts, a
	member does the same if its boot image was made from other source than
	its tree has, `boot_files` beside the image and `(boot-id-tree)`.

*	`waypoint` notes one only when the tree is exactly the commit, by git's
	index, as `git status` first looks, and says what is not. It had said
	there was no commit on every real tree.

## Whiteboard

*	Tried between two machines, the M4 and the x64 Mac. A shape selected on
	one board is held on all, first come first served, and drawn with an
	orange line on the others; no box, tap or hand there takes it, "own
	only" as between users on one board. The paper, the view and the zoom
	are each board's own, one full screen and one a window. The host has a
	switch beside Share, copies, that lets the boards joined to it save a
	copy of the session to their own files, and tells them at once.

## Storage

*	`storage -o`, the store of another machine as a client; `-e id` retires
	a member; `storage-rename`, a path given the object of another in one
	step; who the members are said on a join, before a put, and by `-M`.

## Rack

*	`rack.sh up -f` follows the tree's git branch, updates and builds
	itself, and `rack.bat` does on Windows. A new `snapshot.zip` comes with
	every change to the host, kept under 192KB: the Windows GUI host is
	126KB, without libstdc++'s exceptions.

## Root

*	`lib/files/files.inc`, `lib/task/pipe.inc` and `lib/task/cmd.inc`, with
	the jobs, farm and local they bring, are in the root environment, held
	once in a node for all its tasks: most commands and many apps use them.
	A node holds 96KB more, 2.95MB in place of 2.85MB; files keep their
	`(import)` lines.

*	`(clamp x lo hi)`, and `(path-name path)`, `(path-dir path)`,
	`(path-ext path)` and `(path-stem path)` beside `path-to-absolute`.

*	`check -c struct` also finds a `(structure +x)` whose name root's
	imports have already: Chess's `+job`, now `+chess_job`, could not be
	loaded once `lib/task/jobs.inc` was in root. The worker of a command
	farm, `lib/task/cmd.lisp`, uses the job of `jobs.inc` in place of a copy.

*	`(kernel-stats)` gives a sixth number, the cells in use, `:sys_mem
	:inuse`. The total less what is free moved by the end of a block as a
	heap took one or gave one back, so the leak tests read 6KB more one
	time and less the next of an app that holds nothing: on a slow machine
	the Pi failed them now and then. They read the cells in use, and the
	app test fails an app only if it holds more on each of four runs. Every
	app reads 0 on each of ten.

*	`lib/text/kmplps.inc` is gone, a search that was tried and was never
	quicker than `Substr` or `Regexp`.

*	`(pow)` is of any number, :num, :fixed or :real, by a virtual method.
	`(apply)` gives a native function a list of the elements of a sequence
	that is not one. `reals??`.

## Tools

*	`check` has rules for calls of nothing, `unbound`; an `(inc)` of an
	`(rfind)`, `past`; a logical shift of a difference, `shift`;
	`index`, `collect`, `vpname`, and `args` counts a `(#)`'s `%n` against
	what calls it. `forward` finds no function in the tree used above where
	it is defined.

*	`zip` and `unzip`, `zip-write` in `lib/files/zip.inc`. The magnifier,
	the screen round the mouse in a window. The Launcher folds all and
	opens all, in three columns. `bench` has the media decoders.

## Not ready

*	Opus is not here: WebM's sound is mostly Opus or Vorbis, and its
	pictures VP8, VP9 or AV1, which the host is not given. A film of them
	plays neither.

*	A track of Matroska whose frames had their header stripped, as
	mkvmerge does of MP3 and AC-3, has no sound.

*	A seek in an MP3 of a varying bit rate lands within a few frames of
	the time, as near as its table of a hundred places goes; FLAC of
	blocks of varying sizes is not tested, no encoder here makes it.

*	HE-AAC of more than two channels is not tested.
