# ChrysaLisp 7.4

A draft, what has gone in since the 7.3 tag.

## Audio

*	The microphone. The Audio service sends what it hears, 16 bit mono at
	16000 a second, in pieces of 20ms to whoever listens, and streams play
	a sound as it comes, a voice from far away: the start of voice chat.
	Six new entries in the host's audio table, three for the microphone
	and three for streams, in the SDL3 and ALSA drivers. A listener and a stream
	are held on a lease, so one that has gone lets go of the device. The
	`mic` command. A hidden desktop hears a tone, for the tests.
	[library_primitives.md](../ai_digest/library_primitives.md), "Audio
	Service". New host programs on every machine, Windows too.

*	Sound pipelines, as the stages of an image: a source, filters and a
	sink, each a task, so no whole sound is held. The microphone, a tone and
	a file in; gain, a low pass and a level; a file and the speaker out.
	`lib/audio/`, `.wav` a module of its own as an image's formats are, and
	`stages-run` in `lib/streams/stages.inc`.
	[async_pipelines.md](../ai_digest/async_pipelines.md), "Sound".

*	Voice, `apps/media/voice/`, once Voice Notes: record, stop, play and delete,
	each note named for when it was made, kept in the user's `voice_notes/`.

## Sync

*	A waypoint goes round the herd by itself. Each machine's sync service
	offers the waypoint its tree is exactly at to the others, as it starts,
	when a machine is new to it or back, when it has taken one, and when
	`waypoint` notes one; each takes what is newer and offers it on.
	`service/sync/spread.lisp`, [sync.md](../ai_digest/sync.md), "The herd
	by itself".

*	A tree with no `.git` that is already exactly the tree at a waypoint
	takes it, only the waypoint noted, and a push from a machine it names,
	made from a tree at its waypoint, notes it there too.

*	A machine the herd has taken to a newer waypoint makes itself new. Its
	member ends, and `rack.sh`, which keeps it in a loop, does `make
	install` and starts it again; what was there is put back if the
	install fails. Only the member, its own nodes, and not on a tree with a
	`.git`. [rack.md](../ai_digest/rack.md), "A member". As it starts, a
	member does the same if its boot image was made from other source than
	its tree has, `boot_files` beside the image and `(boot-id-tree)`.

*	`waypoint` notes one only when the tree is exactly the commit, by git's
	index, as `git status` first looks, and says what is not. It had said
	there was no commit on every real tree.

## Whiteboard

*	Tried between two machines, the M4 and the x64 Mac. A shape selected on
	one board is held on all, first come first served, and drawn with an
	orange line on the others; no box, tap or hand there takes it, "own
	only" as between users on one board. The paper, the view and the zoom
	are each board's own, one full screen and one a window. The host has a
	switch beside Share, copies, that lets the boards joined to it save a
	copy of the session to their own files, and tells them at once.

## Root

*	`lib/files/files.inc`, `lib/task/pipe.inc` and `lib/task/cmd.inc`, with
	the jobs, farm and local they bring, are in the root environment, held
	once in a node for all its tasks: most commands and many apps use them.
	A node holds 96KB more, 2.95MB in place of 2.85MB; files keep their
	`(import)` lines.

*	`(clamp x lo hi)`, and `(path-name path)`, `(path-dir path)`,
	`(path-ext path)` and `(path-stem path)` beside `path-to-absolute`.

*	`check -c struct` also finds a `(structure +x)` whose name root's
	imports have already: Chess's `+job`, now `+chess_job`, could not be
	loaded once `lib/task/jobs.inc` was in root. The worker of a command
	farm, `lib/task/cmd.lisp`, uses the job of `jobs.inc` in place of a copy.

*	`(kernel-stats)` gives a sixth number, the cells in use, `:sys_mem
	:inuse`. The total less what is free moved by the end of a block as a
	heap took one or gave one back, so the leak tests read 6KB more one
	time and less the next of an app that holds nothing: on a slow machine
	the Pi failed them now and then. They read the cells in use, and the
	app test fails an app only if it holds more on each of four runs. Every
	app reads 0 on each of ten.

*	`lib/text/kmplps.inc` is gone, a search that was tried and was never
	quicker than `Substr` or `Regexp`.
