# ChrysaLisp 7.7

## Apps

*	Text shows in a Vdu again, the Terminal, Viewer and Editor and the
	code of a Docs page: in 7.6 they were blank. Its configure no longer
	kept the width and height it was given, so it held no characters to
	load or draw.

*	The VP of 64 files is indented by its blocks again, `fmt -i`: the
	checks that were in an `(errorcase)` were left at the margin, or a tab
	short in a loop, when it went in 7.6.

*	The Pcb app routes again: a route stopped at its start with an error,
	the bar's colour set and then asked to be drawn, as if `def` gave
	back the view and not the colour it set.

## Lisp

*	`(read-char stream width)` of a positive width, and `read-short`,
	`read-int` and the like, sign extend by the top bit of the last byte
	read. They went by its lowest bit: `read-short` of `ff 80` gave 33023,
	and of `00 01` gave -65280.

## Tools

*	`make install`, `install.bat` and the install of `rack.sh` and
	`rack.ps1` keep to themselves, as `-a`: an install no longer joins the
	mesh. With the member stopped for an update, the install took its port,
	became the machine's member, and started the machine's store under the
	emulator while it built.

*	`storage -C -v` says, after a mend, that what was wrong was mended, all
	of it or how much, where it said the faults were there still: a cluster
	two have, a chain cut short and one not of the volume it only says.

*	`fmt -i` indents the lines of VP function bodies, and nothing else, by
	their blocks: a line that carries a form on moves with the line it
	opened on. Of `fmt` as of `fmt -i`, a
	`(switch)` is flat: its lines sit where it does till a case, `vpcase`,
	`default` or `else`, which sits there too, and the lines after a case
	are indented.

*	`fmt` leaves a tree that `check` and the tests find as they did. A
	line with a `;check off` comment, and the line after it, are kept as
	they are written. A `(test-cases ...)` has a pair to a line, and its
	forms as they are written. A line that joining left too long, and that
	the rules cannot break, has the break of the source back, as a VP form
	that is never broken did. A break that leaves both lines within the
	limit is made outside an inner form, keeping it whole, before one is
	made inside it. The `symbols` command writes `lib/consts/symbols.inc`
	as `fmt` has it.

*	The source is indented by its structure, `fmt -l 0`, the first step
	to all of it laid out by `fmt`: a line that carries a form on is a tab
	in for each form opened on the line it carries on, and a close bracket
	is on the line before. The line breaks are still as they were written,
	`fmt` laying the lines out is the next step. `lint` ends with
	`fmt -l 0 -c` of all of it, but `usr/` and `tests/scratch/`, so a file
	not so is found.

*	`check` has `def`: a method of what `def` or `set` gives, the last
	value set and not the env, `(. (def view :paper c) :dirty)`. The
	way round is `(def (. view :dirty) :paper c)`.

## VP

*	A tree whose translators are still the vpb_ ones of before 7.6 no longer
	builds its way forward: `make install` brings it up, as from 7.5.

*	`vp-ddef` names the float registers as doubles, `:d0` on, what
	`vp-fdef` did, and `vp-fdef` names them as singles, `:f0` on. A single
	and its double are one register, and a trash of either is a trash of
	both.

*	vpx, the expression compiler of the VP, is gone, and with it
	`def-vars`, `push-scope`, `pop-scope` and `return`. A function's frame
	is a `(structure slot 0 ...)`, made by `(vp-alloc slot_size)` and
	freed by `(vp-free slot_size)`, its fields `(:rsp slot_x)`, many at a
	time by `load-fields` and `save-fields`, the width of each from its
	type as before. `vp-alloc` and `vp-free` round a size up to whole
	longs. The slots of a frame are still kept in registers where they can
	be, `lib/asm/slots.inc` that was `vpxreg.inc`. The vpx documents are
	gone with it. Every function of the tree is written so, most with fewer
	instructions and less going to and from the stack than vpx gave: the
	kernel, the links, the loader, fonts, filled polygons, stroking, the
	image and stream codecs.

*	`assign` throws for memory to memory and a constant to memory, where a
	long of memory to memory had given no instruction at all.

*	A field named `size` of a structure, or `fields` of a frame, throws: it
	is the structure's own name, and was lost to it. The loader's `vp_find`
	had one, and a bind of a function with no native, found from its VP,
	wrote past its frame.

*	`vp-lnot-rr`, `vp-land-rr`, `vp-ext-rr` and the sets, `vp-seq-cr` to
	`vp-sge-rr`, are gone: only vpx made them. Of each translator, the
	VP64 emulator and its translator, their code went with them. An op's
	number is its place in `lib/asm/ops.inc`, so single float ops, which no
	translator's own code uses, took the places they left, and the
	translators make themselves as before. VP64's ops are renumbered, in
	groups now: integer of a constant, of a register, compare and branch,
	memory, calls and jumps, doubles, singles, one list in
	`src/host/vp64.cpp` that `lib/trans/vp64.vp` follows. A VP64 image
	from before does not run on the new host, the new `snapshot.zip` has
	one that does, and a tree that builds its way forward wants
	`make apps` as well as `make all boot platforms`: a VP64 app object
	is made again when its source changes, not its translator.

*	The VP64 emulator's ops each end in a jump to the next of their own,
	in place of one switch, but on Apple Silicon, where the switch is
	the faster, `VP64_SWITCH` in the Makefile. Of the same Lisp, the x64
	Mac 9% faster and the Pi 30%. clang made it a switch again, one
	jump, till `-mllvm -tail-dup-pred-size` and `-tail-dup-succ-size`
	let it copy a block of 182 ways; gcc needs nothing.

*	A shader's native function is named by the translator of its CPU and
	`lib/asm/ops.inc` as well as by the back end, so one kept from before
	a change to them is not run: a VP64 node ran one of the old numbers
	and went down.
