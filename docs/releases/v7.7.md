# ChrysaLisp 7.7

## Apps

*	Text shows in a Vdu again, the Terminal, Viewer and Editor and the
	code of a Docs page: in 7.6 they were blank. Its configure no longer
	kept the width and height it was given, so it held no characters to
	load or draw.

*	The VP of 64 files is indented by its blocks again: the checks that
	were in an `(errorcase)` were left at the margin, or a tab short in a
	loop, when it went in 7.6.

*	The Pcb app routes again: a route stopped at its start with an error,
	the bar's colour set and then asked to be drawn, as if `def` gave
	back the view and not the colour it set.

## Lisp

*	`(read-char stream width)` of a positive width, and `read-short`,
	`read-int` and the like, sign extend by the top bit of the last byte
	read. They went by its lowest bit: `read-short` of `ff 80` gave 33023,
	and of `00 01` gave -65280.

## Tools

*	`make install`, `install.bat` and the install of `rack.sh` and
	`rack.ps1` keep to themselves, as `-a`: an install no longer joins the
	mesh. With the member stopped for an update, the install took its port,
	became the machine's member, and started the machine's store under the
	emulator while it built.

*	`make install`, `make clean`, `install.bat` and the update of
	`rack.ps1` take away `obj/vp64/VP64` before they unpack the snapshot.
	A node makes its own code of a function it binds, from the function's
	VP, and keeps it there for the next to load, and the installer, which
	the emulator runs, had kept the unzip and sum functions of installs
	before. The snapshot's VP64 image has the ops renumbered since 7.6:
	code of the old numbers, loaded as it was found, took the emulator
	down as `waypoint` read `.git`, at the end of every install of a tree
	that had been installed before 7.7, where a fresh clone installed.

*	`storage -C -v` says, after a mend, that what was wrong was mended, all
	of it or how much, where it said the faults were there still: a cluster
	two have, a chain cut short and one not of the volume it only says.

*	`fmt` is a layout engine: it reads a file into its forms and comments
	and lays them out afresh in the house style, so however a file was
	laid out, the same forms give the same text. What is kept of the
	source is its comments, where a blank line was, strings, the help
	text of a command, and what a `;check off` covers. A form that fits
	is on its line, and the line is 120 columns, `-l`, for Lisp and VP
	alike. A form with a test or a body has a width of its own, however
	much room the line has, so the left edge shows the blocks: a `(when)`
	or a loop is a line only up to half the limit, an `(unless)` or a
	`(lambda)` two thirds, an `(if)` `(and)` `(or)` `(each)` three
	quarters, a call, a binding or a list the whole of it, and the test
	of a block that is broken, an `(and)` or `(or)` wider than half the
	limit, has a condition to a line; a definition, a `(cond)`, a `(when)` or a `(lambda)`
	of more than one body form, is broken by its rule; a name and its
	value, and a keyword and its value, are not parted; a form that must
	be over more than one line starts one of its own, and what comes
	after a form over more than one line starts a line. One that is only
	too long hangs on its line, as a `(lambda)` always does, if its first
	argument fits there and no other argument of the form will be over
	more than one line: then each starts a line. A line far in still has
	half the limit past its indent, so code deep in is not made a narrow
	column. Where the tree's authors had a habit it is the rule: the
	clause of `:t` has its first form beside it, a tab in as the rest
	are, an `(if)` that does not fit has its then and else each on a
	line, a `(#)` of two forms that fits is one line, an `(and)` of
	nothing but `(defq)`s has each on a line under the `(and)`, and a
	definition that fits is a line among others, one after another with
	no blank line between, a table of one line definitions as
	`lib/asm/vp.inc` has, where one that stands alone has its body on a
	line of its own. A table,
	a quoted list of atoms or of lists, an `(enum)` or `(bit)`, or a form
	of eight or more atoms, is laid out in rows of its cells, a name and
	its value, a form, or an atom, a power of two of them to a row, the
	same in each part of it, and a part that fits in one row is one row;
	a `(scatter (Fmap) ...)` of names and values is one, and a column, a
	cell to a row, is all under its head. A map of keys and the
	functions they are for, an event map as a key map, is a key and its
	action to a line however much room there is:
	`docs/reference/keys.md` is made the same to the byte.
	The source scanners take a `(` at the very start of a line to open a
	form at the top of a file: each form at the top starts its line and
	every line of what is in one is at least a tab in, so they find what
	they did, and a VP instruction, an `(import)` or an `(ffi)` inside a
	form is laid out as any form is. A declaration is never broken. The
	`includes` command reads a line at a time: a `(call`, `(entry`,
	`(exit`, `(jump` or the like that names a class always starts a
	line, and no line starts with such a word that is not one.
	`fmt -l 0` and `fmt -i` are gone. The `symbols` command writes
	`lib/consts/symbols.inc` as `fmt` has it. The tree itself is not
	laid out by it: `fmt` is set aside for now, and `lint` no longer ends
	with a check of the layout of the source.

*	`check` has `def`: a method of what `def` or `set` gives, the last
	value set and not the env, `(. (def view :paper c) :dirty)`. The
	way round is `(def (. view :dirty) :paper c)`.

## VP

*	A tree whose translators are still the vpb_ ones of before 7.6 no longer
	builds its way forward: `make install` brings it up, as from 7.5.

*	`vp-ddef` names the float registers as doubles, `:d0` on, what
	`vp-fdef` did, and `vp-fdef` names them as singles, `:f0` on. A single
	and its double are one register, and a trash of either is a trash of
	both.

*	vpx, the expression compiler of the VP, is gone, and with it
	`def-vars`, `push-scope`, `pop-scope` and `return`. A function's frame
	is a `(structure slot 0 ...)`, made by `(vp-alloc slot_size)` and
	freed by `(vp-free slot_size)`, its fields `(:rsp slot_x)`, many at a
	time by `load-fields` and `save-fields`, the width of each from its
	type as before. `vp-alloc` and `vp-free` round a size up to whole
	longs. The vpx documents are gone with it. Every function of the tree
	is written so, most with fewer instructions and less going to and from
	the stack than vpx gave: the kernel, the links, the loader, fonts,
	filled polygons, stroking, the image and stream codecs.

*	No pass keeps the slots of a frame in registers: `lib/asm/slots.inc`
	is gone, with what each call noted for it, `method-trashes` and
	`path-method`. A slot is memory, as written, and `vpopt.inc` is the
	only pass over a function's instructions. The pass had changed 42 of
	1,707 functions, 217 stack loads and 74 stores less for 375 register
	copies, 85 instructions and 528 bytes of arm64 more, and it was a
	sixth of a build: `make test` on one node is 0.238 s, was 0.286 s, on
	19 nodes 0.037 s, was 0.042 s. A value wanted again between calls is
	held in a register by the source.

*	31 of those 42 functions are written by hand to do what the pass
	looked for: a value that lives over calls is in a register those
	calls are declared to leave, a call's own result is used in place of
	a reload, and a frame is only what a call that keeps nothing is made
	over. They are 1,770 instructions with 155 stack loads and 108 stores,
	where the source as it was gave 1,958, 335 and 231, and the pass
	2,020, 198 and 181. `defq`, `setq`, the bind of a lambda's args, the
	number reader and the intern of a symbol are among them, with the
	vdu's draw, the film frame decoder, a font's textures, the path
	filters and the streams of Lisp functions. Nine have no frame at all
	now, and a font's `sym_texture` and `ascii_textures` none on their
	usual path. The arm64 code of the tree is 1,204 bytes less than with
	the pass.

*	`assign` throws for memory to memory and a constant to memory, where a
	long of memory to memory had given no instruction at all.

*	A field named `size` of a structure, or `fields` of a frame, throws: it
	is the structure's own name, and was lost to it. The loader's `vp_find`
	had one, and a bind of a function with no native, found from its VP,
	wrote past its frame.

*	`vp-lnot-rr`, `vp-land-rr`, `vp-ext-rr` and the sets, `vp-seq-cr` to
	`vp-sge-rr`, are gone: only vpx made them. Of each translator, the
	VP64 emulator and its translator, their code went with them. An op's
	number is its place in `lib/asm/ops.inc`, so single float ops, which no
	translator's own code uses, took the places they left, and the
	translators make themselves as before. VP64's ops are renumbered, in
	groups now: integer of a constant, of a register, compare and branch,
	memory, calls and jumps, doubles, singles, one list in
	`src/host/vp64.cpp` that `lib/trans/vp64.vp` follows. A VP64 image
	from before does not run on the new host, the new `snapshot.zip` has
	one that does, and a tree that builds its way forward wants
	`make apps` as well as `make all boot platforms`: a VP64 app object
	is made again when its source changes, not its translator.

*	The VP64 emulator's ops each end in a jump to the next of their own,
	in place of one switch, but on Apple Silicon, where the switch is
	the faster, `VP64_SWITCH` in the Makefile. Of the same Lisp, the x64
	Mac 9% faster and the Pi 30%. clang made it a switch again, one
	jump, till `-mllvm -tail-dup-pred-size` and `-tail-dup-succ-size`
	let it copy a block of 182 ways; gcc needs nothing.

*	A shader's native function is named by the translator of its CPU and
	`lib/asm/ops.inc` as well as by the back end, so one kept from before
	a change to them is not run: a VP64 node ran one of the old numbers
	and went down.
