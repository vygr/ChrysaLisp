# ChrysaLisp 7.8

## Apps

*	The MNIST app is Digits, `apps/science/digits`: it is named for what
	it learns, as the next networks of `lib/nn` will be, and MNIST is the
	data set it learns them from, whose names keep it. Its settings are
	kept in `digits.tre`, the rate, batch and back end chosen under the
	old name are not carried over.

*	What an app downloads is kept in `downloads/` at the top of the
	tree, a folder for each thing fetched, which git does not track:
	Digits keeps MNIST in `downloads/mnist/`, for any app that wants it,
	and Model each file of a model it fetches in `downloads/models/`, by
	its address, so a model picked again comes from there: the Spider, its
	`.obj`, `.mtl` and five pictures, took 3.2 s the first time, 6 ms the
	next.
	A tree that pulls this keeps the data set it fetched before in
	`apps/science/mnist/data/`, which git did not track either, and the
	app fetches it again: the old folder can be deleted.

*	Surface and Raymarch shade their CPU frames by a herd on the nodes of
	this machine, a child a node, kept off the window's own node. Their
	farms had started children on any node of the network: with another
	machine in it, a Pi or an x64, its tiles sent their pixels back by
	mail where those here shade straight into the shared ones, and every
	frame waited for its last tile, 5 s a frame of Surface on the M4 with
	the x64 and a Pi in the network. A tile is one long call of native
	code, and kept off the window's node it no longer holds the window up
	while it runs, nor while the shader is first assembled.

## Lisp

*	`(tanh num)`, of a fixed, a double or a float, and `tanh` of the
	shader language, on every back end. Of `:sys_math :d_tanh` and
	`:f_tanh`, each worked out at its own width, a single's in 32 bits by
	`:f_exp`, the first function of `sys/math` of singles: below 0.55
	the series of e to the twice of it less 1, to the 19th power of a
	double and the 11th of a single, and above, by exp. Of 858 numbers
	from -25 to 25 each is at the most 2 units of its last place from the
	true tanh, 680 of the doubles and 684 of the singles the nearest. The
	VP back end of the shader language calls `:f_tanh` as it is, so a
	kernel's tanh there is Lisp's to the bit, and the GPU's own is near
	it. A fixed's is by `:d_tanh`, to its 16 bits.

*	The classes of numbers are a ladder of what each can do, `:num`,
	`:fixed`, `:double`, `:float`, and of vectors `:nums`, `:fixeds`,
	`:doubles`, `:floats`: a float comes of a double, as every float is
	exactly a double, and floats of doubles, where both came of fixed
	point. Fixed point keeps what drawing works in, `frac` `floor` `ceil`
	`recip` `sin` `cos`; `exp`, `log` and `tanh` are of a double and a
	float only, an error of a fixed, and `:sys_math :q_exp` and `:q_log`,
	of no other use, are gone. `(double? x)` is true of a float, as
	`(fixed? x)` of a double is; `(double?? x)` asks what x is exactly,
	and what reads a double as stored asks that, the VP functions of
	`lib/nn` and `lib/media` taking exactly `:DOUBLE` and `:DOUBLES`.
	`docs/lisp/numerics.md` is the one document of numbers now, the
	digest's merged into it.

## VP

*	The functions of `sys/math` of a number, `d_` `f_` and `q_`, a value
	in a register in and out, keep to one set of what they may trash,
	`:r0` to `:r2` and `:d0` to `:d3`, `sys/math/trashes.inc`, so what
	calls them, the shader compiler and the classes of numbers, keeps what
	it wants in any other register across the call. `trace -l`, and so
	`lint`, says of one that goes outside it. `:d_pow`, which trashed
	`:d0` to `:d7`, keeps to it now: the double waits in `:d3` and the
	whole part of the power in `:r2`, the 1 of the roots in `:d3`, so
	none of it is memory, every power the same to the bit, 1226 of them
	compared. The shader compiler spills that one set around a call of
	`sys/math`, where it had two sets of its own and spilled every
	register around `pow`: of the 21 shaders of the tree, 170 moves to
	and from the stack are 148, Eyes and the atom of Molecule none.
